Install Flow

About 5 minutes. You only do this once.

What you need

  • An invite link from James. It opens a page with these same steps and a button that joins you. It works once and lasts 7 days.
  • Your own Google account (Gmail and Calendar).

That's all. The invite brings everything else Flow needs.

The steps

Open the invite link James sent you. The page it opens has these steps with your invite built in, so Flow joins by itself.

Windows

  1. Click Get Flow, then Flow-Setup-…exe on the page that opens.
  2. Open the file. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Your browser opens: sign in to Google. If Google says "hasn't verified this app": Advanced, Go to Flow, allow what it lists.

Mac

  1. Open Terminal: Cmd + Space, type Terminal, Return.
  2. Paste the line from the invite page and press Return. Wait about 5 minutes; Flow opens by itself.
  3. If your Mac asks about "Flow Safe Storage": your Mac password, then Always Allow.
  4. Your browser opens: sign in to Google, as above.

Flow then opens on your list and reads your last few days of email; tasks appear within a couple of minutes. Your name comes from the invite; change it, and everything else, in Settings. If Flow opens on Welcome to Flow without joining, the invite page has a button and the invite to paste.

Flow on your phone (optional)

Flow has a website for your phone. Your lists and drafts work any time. Email, Claude and sending work while your computer is on with Flow running.

  1. In Flow: Settings, Connections, Flow website, Connect a phone. A QR code appears for 10 minutes.
  2. Scan it with your phone's camera and open the link.
  3. The first time, tap First time? Make your login: your own email and a new password, for the Flow website only.
  4. When it asks Use Face ID next time?, say yes.

Want an app icon? In Safari: Share, Add to Home Screen. The first time you open it from there it asks for the link from your laptop: click Connect a phone again, then Copy the link, send it to your phone and paste it in.

Keep the computer plugged in with its lid open. Flow keeps it awake while your phone is connected. If it sleeps, the phone says your laptop isn't answering; your lists still work.

Updating

  • Windows: Flow updates itself.
  • Mac: Flow tells you when a new version is out. Click the F at the top of the screen, then Update to Flow …. It takes about 5 minutes and Flow restarts by itself. If Flow can't read your texts afterwards: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Full Disk Access, select Flow, click the minus, then add Flow again with the plus.

Good to know

  • Nothing is sent without your click. Flow only prepares emails and messages; you check them and press Send.
  • Want to try it safely first? Tick Practice mode in Settings, Connections. Nothing leaves at all.
  • Closing the window keeps Flow running (the F at the top of a Mac screen, or by the clock on Windows). Quit it from there.
  • Where your things are kept: your tasks, drafts and attached files are in a database James runs for his friends, in a section only your Flow can reach. Your email and your Google sign-in stay on your own computer. See privacy.
