Install Flow

About 20 minutes. You only do this once.

Before you start

James gives you four things. Keep the last three private: don't email them or post them anywhere.

  1. Flow itself: a zip file (Mac) or Flow Setup file (Windows).
  2. The database line: one long line starting postgresql://
  3. A Claude key: starts sk-ant-
  4. The Google file: a small file ending .json

You also need your own Claude subscription (Pro or Max, at claude.ai). Flow does its regular work on it.

Mac

  1. Unzip the Flow zip in your Downloads folder. You get a folder called Flow.
  2. Open Terminal: press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  3. Paste this line and press Return: 
    bash ~/Downloads/Flow/install-mac.sh
    If your Mac asks whether Terminal can access your Downloads folder, click Allow. Wait about 5 minutes. Flow opens by itself when it's done.
  4. Install Claude Code: in the same Terminal, paste this line and press Return: 
    curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash
  5. Sign in to Claude Code: type claude and press Return. Choose to sign in with your Claude account and follow the steps in the browser. Then type /exit and press Return.
  6. Now connect Flow (below).

Your Mac asks a few questions the first time. "Flow Safe Storage": type your Mac password and click Always Allow. "Flow wants to control Word", Messages or a folder: click OK or Allow.

Updating later: move the old Flow folder in Downloads to the Bin, unzip the new zip, and paste the same line again. If Flow can't read your texts afterwards: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Full Disk Access, select Flow, click the minus, then add Flow again with the plus.

Windows

  1. Double-click the Flow Setup file.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Flow installs and opens.
  4. Install Claude Code: press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter. Paste this line and press Enter: 
    irm https://claude.ai/install.ps1 | iex
  5. Sign in to Claude Code: close PowerShell and open it again. Type claude, press Enter, sign in with your Claude account in the browser, then type /exit.
  6. Now connect Flow (below).

Connect Flow

Flow opens on Connections. Go down the page:

  1. Database: paste the database line, click Connect.
  2. Claude: paste the Claude key, click Save.
  3. Google: click Load from file… and pick the Google file. Click Connect Google and sign in with your own Google account. Google says "Google hasn't verified this app": click Advanced, then Go to Flow, then allow everything it lists.
  4. About you: type your full name and a sentence about your work. Claude writes in your name. A minute after Google is connected, Flow reads your last 40 sent emails once and writes a short note on how you write. It shows here, and you can change it.
  5. Background work: tick Use Claude Code (my Claude subscription) for the mail check, then click Save options.
  6. Click Open Flow. Done.

Flow on your phone (optional)

Flow has a website for your phone. Your lists and drafts work any time. Email, Claude and sending work while your computer is on with Flow running.

  1. James sets up your website login with you: your email and a password you choose. He also gives you one more key, starting sb_publishable_.
  2. In Flow: Settings, Connections, Flow website. Paste that key and click Save.
  3. Click Connect a phone. A QR code appears for 10 minutes.
  4. Scan it with your phone's camera, open the link, and sign in with your email and password.
  5. When it asks Use Face ID next time?, say yes.

Want an app icon? In Safari: Share, Add to Home Screen. The first time you open it from there it asks for the link from your laptop: click Connect a phone again, then Copy the link, send it to your phone and paste it in.

Keep the computer plugged in with its lid open. Flow keeps it awake while your phone is connected. If it sleeps, the phone says your laptop isn't answering; your lists still work.

Good to know

  • Nothing is sent without your click. Flow only prepares emails and messages; you check them and press Send.
  • Want to try it safely first? Tick Practice mode in Settings, Connections. Nothing leaves at all.
  • Closing the window keeps Flow running (the F at the top of a Mac screen, or by the clock on Windows). Quit it from there.
