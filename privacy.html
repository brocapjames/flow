Flow

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Flow is a personal desktop app for Windows, used privately by its developer. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What Flow accesses

When you connect your Google account, Flow can read and manage your Gmail (to find what needs a reply, prepare drafts, and send emails you approve) and your Google Calendar (to show your week and create events you approve).

How the data is used

  • Only to show your to-do list and prepare next steps for you.
  • Emails and events are only sent or created when you click to approve them.
  • Email text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to summarise it and draft replies. Anthropic doesn't use API data to train its models.

Where it's stored

Your to-do list and drafts are stored in a private database belonging to the app's owner. Sign-in keys are stored encrypted on your own computer. Nothing is stored on this website.

Sharing

Flow doesn't sell, rent or share Google user data. It isn't used for advertising, and people don't read it except the account owner.

Flow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Flow at any time in Flow's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored sign-in keys.
