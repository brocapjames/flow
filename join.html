Install it, sign in to Google, done. About 5 minutes.

This page needs the whole link James sent you. Open that link again, or ask him for a new one.

On Windows

  1. Get FlowA download page opens. Click Flow-Setup-…exe.
  2. Open the file you downloaded."Windows protected your PC"? Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Sign in to Google when your browser opens."Google hasn't verified this app"? Click Advanced, then Go to Flow, and allow what it lists.

That's it. Flow joins by itself and opens on your list.

On a Mac

  1. Open Terminal.Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  2. Paste this line and press Return. 
    curl -fsSL https://brocapjames.github.io/flow/mac.sh | bash
    Wait about 5 minutes. Flow opens by itself.
  3. Click Always Allow if your Mac asks about "Flow Safe Storage".It asks for your Mac password once.
  4. Sign in to Google when your browser opens."Google hasn't verified this app"? Click Advanced, then Go to Flow, and allow what it lists.

That's it. Flow joins by itself and opens on your list.
