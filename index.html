Flow

A personal to-do app for Windows that works from your own email and calendar.

What it does

  • Reads your Gmail and Google Calendar, with your permission, to keep a to-do list of what needs your attention.
  • Suggests a next step for each item, such as a draft reply or a meeting time, for you to check.
  • Only sends an email or creates a calendar event when you click to approve it.

Who it's for

Flow is a private app used by its developer. It isn't sold or offered to the public.
